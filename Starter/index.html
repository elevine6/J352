Meet Nick Polinsky

Where it Started

Nick Polinsky is always eager to be involved in the action. He has had a passion for play-by-play broadcasting since early high school, which led him to immerse himself in the sports journalism scene immediately after arriving at the University of Maryland.

Polinsky’s love for the game stems from his undying loyalty to the Baltimore Ravens. He has attended over 90 games.

His need to be involved extends to other aspects of his life beyond sports. Polinsky’s dream superpower would be invisibility, so he can listen to other people’s conversations.

“I’m a pretty nosy person,” said Polinsky.
