Changes in Navient Complaints from 2023-2024

By Emma Levine
April 20, 2026

Following the change in ownership of Navient in 2021, many the transition led to an increase in loan servicing complaints. Consumer complaints increased from 2023 to 2024, especially in certain states.

Complaints in California, for example, increased greatly, from 73 complaints in 2023 to 460 in 2024. However, other states like New York saw a smaller increase, from 66 complaints in 2023 to 150 complaints in 2024.

The majority of issues that consumers complained about was issues dealing with a lender or servicer, making up 59.1% of the types of issues consumers complained about. The second most complained about issue was struggling to repay loans, at 27.6%.
